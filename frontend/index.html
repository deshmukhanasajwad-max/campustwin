Upload a timetable

CSV or Excel · each row is one weekly session
Columns: Subject Faculty Class Type (Class or Lab) Day Time (e.g. 09:00-11:00) and optionally Room and Building. A Lab column (yes/no, or the lab's room ID) also works.
Nothing is booked when you upload. Once an administrator approves the timetable, the requested rooms and labs are accepted and booked for every week between the two dates.
